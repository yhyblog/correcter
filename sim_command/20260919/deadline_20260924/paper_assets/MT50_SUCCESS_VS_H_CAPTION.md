**Figure caption.** Observed MT50 success across execution horizons. (a) Success rates for N, Post, and Velocity at H = 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50; R is shown only at its observed H = 10 setting. (b) Post and Velocity success gains relative to N at the same H, in percentage points. The raw plan length is fixed at 50 steps; H denotes the native execution/replanning interval, not the generated plan length. Each observed point aggregates 50 tasks × 10 source episodes (500 completed episodes); with equal task counts, the pooled rate also equals the mean of the 50 task success rates. D, G0, and Q remain frozen at their five-task versions. The intervention commitment length is fixed at 10 steps (C10), with at most one intervention per episode. Markers show observed means, and connecting segments are visual guides. No confidence intervals or significance claims are shown. R has no observations at the other horizons and is not extrapolated.
